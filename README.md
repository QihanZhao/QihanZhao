### About me

I'm an M.Phil. student at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, working on **generalizable physical interaction skills for humanoid robots** through imitation learning, reinforcement learning, and physics-based simulation.

I received my **B.Eng. in Computer Science** from **Central South University**, graduating **1st in my major (1/235)**, and was awarded the **National Scholarship**.

> 📍 **CoRL 2026 · Austin** — Presenting **HumanX**. Happy to connect and talk about humanoid learning and physical interaction!
