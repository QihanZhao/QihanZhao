### About me

Hi! I'm **Qihan Zhao (HanX)**, an M.Phil. student at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**. My research focuses on **learning generalizable physical interaction skills for humanoid robots**, especially through imitation learning, reinforcement learning, and physics-based simulation.

I'm particularly interested in how robots can acquire **versatile, contact-rich interaction capabilities** that extend beyond narrowly specified tasks and demonstrations.

> 📍 **CoRL 2026 · Austin** — Presenting **HumanX**. I'd be happy to chat about humanoid learning, physical interaction, and robotics research!

### Selected research

#### 🏀 HumanX: Toward Agile and Generalizable Humanoid Interaction Skills from Human Videos

**CoRL 2026** · **Equal contribution**

<a href="https://wyhuai.github.io/human-x/"><img src="https://wyhuai.github.io/human-x/static/images/teaser_0.png" alt="HumanX: from human videos to humanoid interaction skills" width="900" /></a>

A framework for turning human videos into agile, transferable humanoid interaction skills, combining motion/data generation with unified imitation learning. Demonstrated on real humanoid robots across diverse interactions.

[**Project Page**](https://wyhuai.github.io/human-x/) · [**Paper**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02473) · [**Code**](https://github.com/wyhuai/HumanX)

#### 🤖 SkillMimic-V2: Learning Robust and Generalizable Interaction Skills from Sparse and Noisy Demonstrations

**SIGGRAPH 2025** · **Equal contribution**

Learning robust interaction skills from imperfect demonstrations using trajectory stitching, state-transition modeling, and adaptive sampling for improved skill transitions and recovery.

[**Project Page**](https://ingrid789.github.io/SkillMimicV2/) · [**Paper**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02094) · [**Code**](https://github.com/Ingrid789/SkillMimic-V2) · [**Video**](https://youtu.be/jk94jXUsyhE)

#### ⛹️ SkillMimic: Learning Basketball Interaction Skills from Demonstrations

**CVPR 2025 Highlight** · **Equal contribution**

A unified imitation-learning approach for acquiring diverse basketball interaction skills from human-object demonstrations and reusing them for more complex, long-horizon tasks.

[**Project Page**](https://ingrid789.github.io/SkillMimic/) · [**Paper**](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Wang_SkillMimic_Learning_Basketball_Interaction_Skills_from_Demonstrations_CVPR_2025_paper.html) · [**Code**](https://github.com/wyhuai/SkillMimic) · [**Video**](https://youtu.be/j1smsXilUGM)
