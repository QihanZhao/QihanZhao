### About me

Hi! I'm **Qihan Zhao**, an M.Phil. student at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**. My research focuses on learning **generalizable physical interaction skills for humanoid robots**, particularly through imitation learning, reinforcement learning, and physics-based simulation. I'm interested in enabling robots to acquire versatile, contact-rich interaction capabilities beyond narrowly specified tasks and demonstrations.

I received my **B.Eng. in Computer Science** from **Central South University**, graduating **1st in my major (1/235)**, and was awarded the **National Scholarship**.

> 📍 **CoRL 2026 · Austin** — Presenting **HumanX**. Happy to connect and discuss humanoid learning, physical interaction, and robotics research!

### Selected research

1. **[HumanX: Toward Agile and Generalizable Humanoid Interaction Skills from Human Videos](https://wyhuai.github.io/human-x/)**  
   **CoRL 2026** · *Equal contribution*

   A full-stack framework for learning agile, transferable humanoid interaction skills from human videos, without task-specific reward engineering.

   [Project Page](https://wyhuai.github.io/human-x/) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02473) · [Code](https://github.com/wyhuai/HumanX)

2. **[Switch: Learning Agile Skills Switching for Humanoid Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14834)**  
   **ICRA 2026** · *Equal contribution*

   A hierarchical multi-skill system combining a skill graph, reinforcement-learned whole-body tracking, and online scheduling for smooth transitions between dynamic humanoid skills.

   [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14834)

3. **[SkillMimic-V2: Learning Robust and Generalizable Interaction Skills from Sparse and Noisy Demonstrations](https://ingrid789.github.io/SkillMimicV2/)**  
   **SIGGRAPH 2025** · *Equal contribution*

   Robust interaction learning from imperfect demonstrations, with augmented skill transitions and recovery behaviors.

   [Project Page](https://ingrid789.github.io/SkillMimicV2/) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02094) · [Code](https://github.com/Ingrid789/SkillMimic-V2) · [Video](https://youtu.be/jk94jXUsyhE)

4. **[SkillMimic: Learning Basketball Interaction Skills from Demonstrations](https://ingrid789.github.io/SkillMimic/)**  
   **CVPR 2025 Highlight** · *Equal contribution*

   A unified imitation-learning approach for acquiring reusable basketball interaction skills from human-object demonstrations and composing them into long-horizon tasks.

   [Project Page](https://ingrid789.github.io/SkillMimic/) · [Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Wang_SkillMimic_Learning_Basketball_Interaction_Skills_from_Demonstrations_CVPR_2025_paper.html) · [Code](https://github.com/wyhuai/SkillMimic) · [Video](https://youtu.be/j1smsXilUGM)
